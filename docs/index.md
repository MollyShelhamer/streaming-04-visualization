## Custom Project

### Dataset

- Uses `data/sales.csv`.
- Contains sales order records for course product purchases.
- Each record includes fields such as `order_id`, `customer_id`,
  `datetime`, `product_id`, `region_id`, `unit_price`, `quantity`,
  `discount_code`, `payment_method`, `device_type`, `referral_source`,
  `is_online`, `is_new_customer`, `currency_code`, and `customer_note`.
- I used the original sales dataset, not a modified copy.

### Kafka Messages

- The producer sends one validated sales record per Kafka message.
- Messages are sent to the topic
  `streaming-04-visualization-shelhamer`.
- The producer uses `region_id` as the message key.
- I did not change the message fields themselves; I kept the original
  sales fields and validated them before sending.

### Consumer Processing

- The consumer receives sale messages from Kafka and processes each one.
- It consumes all available messages until the timeout or max message limit
  is reached.
- It logs each record and running statistics such as total sales, average,
  minimum, and maximum.
- It writes processed records to `data/output/consumed_sales.csv`.
- It validates required sales fields, enriches messages with derived fields
  like subtotal, tax amount, and total, and then saves the selected fields.

### Experiments

- Phase 4: I updated the topic name from
  `streaming-04-visualization-case` to
  `streaming-04-visualization-shelhamer` in `.env` and `README.md`,
  recreated the Kafka topic, and reran the producer and consumer.
- Phase 5: I added a new sales-by-region chart in
  `src/streaming/kafka_consumer_shelhamer.py` to show cumulative totals by
  region.

### Results

- The producer and consumer ran successfully with the new topic name.
- The workflow generated output files in `data/output` and displayed live
  charts while consuming messages.
- The new region chart showed aggregated sales totals by region alongside
  the existing live sales chart.

### Interpretation

- The custom change updated the example to use my own topic name and made
  the stream more personal.
- Watching messages move through Kafka showed how records are validated,
  enriched, and turned into analytics in real time.
- The stream can show a business which regions are contributing sales as
  messages arrive.
- This workflow adds business intelligence by tracking region-level sales
  distribution and sales velocity from the consumed messages.
